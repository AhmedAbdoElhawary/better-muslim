01

Signature concept: the Living Sky

The home screen is a window onto the user's own sky. Six anchor states are interpolated continuously from their real prayer times, and the sun or moon travels a single arc that doubles as progress between prayers. The symbol is built on the same idea: an eight-point star used as a window.

03

Logo

The symbol is a khatam (8-point star) treated as a window. Inside it, a horizon and the sun's arc, with the sun at the time of Asr. In product, the window fills with the live sky. It is drawn from one line weight so it can animate as a single stroke.

05

Alternative names

"Better Muslim" is direct but describes the user rather than the experience, and can read as a judgement. Three single-word Arabic names that work in every target language, each with a logo sketch.

Falakفلك
"The orbit, the celestial sphere"

Classical Arabic for the path of the sun and moon. Names the Living Sky directly. Two syllables, easy to say in Urdu, Turkish, Malay and French.

Sketch: an orbit with the sun on it, a small star at the centre.

Sakinaسكينة
"Tranquility, inner calm"

Names the feeling the app is designed for. Warm and familiar across the Muslim world; also a common given name, which makes it personal but harder to own in search.

Sketch: a mihrab arch reflected in still water.

Miqatميقات
"The appointed time"

Anchors the brand in prayer times, the feature used five times a day. Also the term for the Hajj boundary stations, which may compete in search.

Sketch: a sundial with eight hours and a gold shadow.

All three need App Store / Play Store availability and trademark checks in key markets before you commit.

06

Typography

An inscriptional Latin serif and a geometric Kufi share the same carved, architectural quality for display. Both body faces are quiet and highly legible at small sizes. Quran text has its own dedicated Mushaf face and is never set in a UI font.

10

Illustration style

Architecture, horizon, sky and light, built from flat gradient planes and a single gold line. These are direction sketches; final illustrations will be produced as vector and Lottie.

Window · onboarding, location permission
Horizon · Maghrib, iftar, travel mode
Night architecture · Isha, Ramadan, last ten nights
Light from the skyEvery scene is lit by one of the six sky states, matched to the moment it appears.
Three planes at mostSky, architecture or land, and one gold line. No textures, no shading.
No figuresNo people or animals, including silhouettes. Presence is implied by lit windows.
No sacred textQuran text never appears in illustration, pattern or texture.
11

Voice and tone

A calm friend who keeps time. Warm, brief, never preachy. We remind, we don't judge. Religious content is cited, not paraphrased.

Gentle
No exclamation marks in reminders. No urgency, no countdown pressure in copy.
Encouraging
Point to what's possible next. Missed prayers lead to a path, never a penalty.
Brief
One idea per message. Notifications under 60 characters.
Humble
We don't issue rulings. Hadith always show source and grading; settings explain, not prescribe.

12

Sound direction

The human voice for the adhan, natural materials for everything else. No music, no melodic jingles. UI sounds are off by default and never play over recitation.
