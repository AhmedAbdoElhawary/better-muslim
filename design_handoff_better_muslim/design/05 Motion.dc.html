MOTION · #9 QIYAM EMERALD

Motion

Sixteen animations, all built from the Phase 2 motion tokens. At rest the app moves slowly, like the sky. When you act, it answers quickly and settles. Every animation has a reduce-motion fallback, and haptics are reserved for moments of completion and alignment. Click any demo to replay it from the start. Tweaks: playback speed and reduce motion.

04

Haptics

Haptics mark completion, alignment and counting. Ambient motion, the countdown, sky changes and navigation are silent to the hand. A Haptics setting (default on) sits under Settings › Sound and touch, and the OS system-haptics switch always wins.

05

Choreography and performance

Rules for when animations meet each other, the user's hand, or a low battery.

PriorityAdhan, then completions (08, 10, 12), then responsive, then ambient. A higher tier pauses lower ones; ambient resumes with a 300 ms fade.
One ceremony at a timeIf two land together, for example the fifth prayer is marked as the Isha adhan begins, the adhan plays and Day complete shows on return to the Prayer tab.
Never queue, always retargetA new tap during a spring retargets it from its current position and velocity. Fast tasbih counting never builds a backlog.
Input stays liveNo animation blocks touch. Ceremonial overlays dismiss on any tap, and the launch sequence skips to Home once data is ready.
Frame budgetLiving Sky repaints at 30 fps inside a RepaintBoundary. Everything else runs at the display rate, with no layout work during animation.
BatteryAmbient loops stop when the app is in the background or in Low Power Mode / Battery Saver. Sky changes still happen, as a 300 ms crossfade.
06

Reduce motion

One flag drives every fallback: the OS setting or the in-app switch. Turn on Reduce motion in Tweaks to see each demo above play its fallback.

No travelTranslation, scale, rotation and clip reveals become crossfades of 150–300 ms. Springs become standard 300 ms with no overshoot.
No loopsLiving Sky, twinkle and the prayer glow freeze on the current sky state. Sky changes still happen, as a 300 ms crossfade.
Meaning staysState changes stay visible: the check still fills, the dial still turns accent at alignment, and haptics still fire.
// lib/theme/motion.dart
extension BmMotionX on BuildContext {
  bool get reduceMotion =>
      MediaQuery.disableAnimationsOf(this) || BmPrefs.of(this).reduceMotion;

  /// Travel animations collapse to a short fade.
  Duration motion(Duration d) => reduceMotion ? BmMotion.micro : d;
  Curve curve(Curve c) => reduceMotion ? Curves.linear : c;
}

// usage
AnimatedSwitcher(
  duration: context.motion(BmMotion.standard),
  transitionBuilder: context.reduceMotion
      ? (c, a) => FadeTransition(opacity: a, child: c)
      : BmTransitions.sharedAxisY,
  child: Text(nextPrayer.name, key: ValueKey(nextPrayer)),
);
