05

Engine rules

What the calculator does, in the order it does it. These rules become the zakat engine and its tests in the handoff. Positions follow the majority view with the common differences exposed as settings; the app states that it is not a fatwa.

net = Σ counted assets − Σ deductible debts
due = net ≥ nisab ? roundHalfUp(net × 0.025) : 0
sample: 45,533.40 × 0.025 = 1,138.335 → £1,138.34 · Hanafi with jewellery: 48,854.40 → £1,221.36
Rate
2.5% of net zakatable wealth for a lunar year (354 days). If the user picks a solar year the rate is 2.577%.
Nisab
Silver 595 g or gold 85 g of pure metal at the live price on the zakat date. Silver by default. Below nisab, nothing is due.
Zakat year
One zakat date per user (Hijri). The year starts the day wealth first reaches nisab. Money gained during the year joins the same pool and is counted on the date.
Cash
All bank balances, cash and e-money on the zakat date. Foreign currency at the spot rate. Interest received is excluded and flagged for giving away.
Gold and silver
Weight × purity × price. 24k 1.000, 22k .916, 21k .875, 18k .750, 14k .585; silver 999 or 925. Worn jewellery: exempt (Shafiʿi, Maliki, Hanbali), counted (Hanafi).
Investments
Trading shares and funds at market value. Long-term: full market value (default) or zakatable company assets per share. Crypto at market value. Pensions only when accessible.
Business
Stock at sale value + business cash + good receivables − payables due now. Equipment, premises and vehicles excluded.
Debts
Owed to you and expected back: counted. Doubtful: counted when received. Debts you owe that are due now: deducted (setting). Long-term debt: next 12 months only, off by default.
Crops
Per crop, nisab 653 kg (5 wasq). 10% rain-fed, 5% irrigated, 7.5% mixed. Due at harvest, no year.
Livestock
Free-grazing animals held a year, by the tables below. Animals for sale count as business stock.
Found wealth
Rikaz 20% when found. Mined gold and silver 2.5% when extracted.
Zakat al-Fitr
Per person supported: 1 saʿ of staple food (about 2.5–3 kg) or the local value set by the user’s mosque or zakat body. Before the Eid prayer.
Rounding
All money in minor units (pence). Round half up once, at the end. Display in the user’s currency and locale; Arabic uses Eastern digits and ٬ ٫.
