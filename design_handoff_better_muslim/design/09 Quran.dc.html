The Mushaf

A reader that behaves like the printed Mushaf: one page at a time, the real Madinah layout, turned like paper. Everything else, recitation, tafsir, words, memorising, khatmah and sharing, sits one tap from the ayah you are reading and never covers the text. Quran text in these mockups is Al-Fatiha only; other pages show neutral line placeholders until the QCF dataset is wired.

4.21Page controls

Controls slide in on glass and the page springs back to a card, so no control ever sits over Quran text. The next page peeks on the left (RTL). Scrubber runs right to left, with juz ticks.

4.31Display settings

“Follow the sky” is the default: paper from Fajr, night after Isha. Book turn is a 3D page leaf; Slide is the plain 300 ms page slide. Reduce motion forces a 150 ms crossfade.

05

Reader rules

Reading model
Page-first (Madinah 604-page Mushaf, Hafs). Translation view (4.07) is the second layout. Both share position: switching keeps the same ayah on screen.
Page render
Production: QCF V2 per-page fonts (604 fonts, lazy-downloaded, cached) with glyph-level line layout, so every line matches the printed Mushaf. Mockups: Amiri Quran.
Hit testing
Each glyph knows its ayah (from QCF word data). Tap = select ayah. Long-press = select word. Hit areas are whole line-height bands, never smaller than 44 pt tall.
Page color
Follow the sky (default), Paper, Cream, Night. Reader chrome takes the page palette, not the app mode.
Selection
gold wash rgba(168,138,82,.26) on paper, .22 on night. Follow wash: primary 10% on paper, accent 16% on night. Radius 9, never recolors the glyphs.
Audio
Gapless per-ayah files with word timing. Background playback, lock screen controls, AirPlay / Cast. Repeat ayah N, range, pause between ayahs, speed .75–1.5, sleep timer.
Offline
Text, fonts and the default tafsir and translation ship in the app (about 40 MB). Reciters and extra tafsir download per surah or in full.
Sync
Last read, bookmarks, notes, highlights, khatmah and memorisation sync through the account backend; work offline-first.
