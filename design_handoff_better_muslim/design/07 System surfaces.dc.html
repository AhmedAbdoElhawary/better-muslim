System surfaces

Where the app reaches people without being opened: notifications, the Live Activity and Dynamic Island, and widgets. The same scenario as the screens: Thursday 15 October, 16:32 in Makkah, Maghrib next. Every countdown on this page is live. Surfaces stay dark regardless of theme, because they sit over wallpapers the app doesn't control.

01

One notification per prayer, sent at the exact time with the sound chosen in onboarding. The reminder and azkar nudges are quieter. Nothing is promotional, and nothing nags about missed prayers.
