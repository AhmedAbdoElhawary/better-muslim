# Android plays its own notification sounds

ColorOS (OPPO, OnePlus, realme) mutes every channel sound of an app unless its "Ringtone" switch on the app's notification page is on, the `app_ringtone_allow` flag in `NotificationManagerService.buzzBeepBlinkLocked`. It is off by default and the Android 13 notification dialog never turns it on, so the azkar voice, the reminder's takbir and the tones were silent on my OPPO while the full adhan, played by `AdhanService`, worked. Changing channel ids twice didn't help, it is the wrong layer. So on Android every channel is silent and `packages/adhan_alarms` plays the sound itself when the alarm fires: the takbir or tone with the countdown, the tone at the prayer time, the set's name with the nudge. It plays on notification volume, and nothing plays in silent, vibrate or do not disturb, or when the notification or its channel is off. The nudges moved to `adhan_alarms` too, with exact alarms, not alarm clocks. iOS keeps its notification sounds. Vibration still comes from the channel, ColorOS has a "Vibrate" switch with the same default, I left it.
