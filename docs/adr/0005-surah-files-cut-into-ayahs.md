# Surah files cut into ayahs, a second recitation source

Partly replaces 0001. Ahmad Al-Nufais, Hassan Mohamed Saleh and Haitham Al-Dukhain aren't on EveryAyah, mp3quran.net has them as one mp3 per surah with each ayah's start and end in ms. The player still plays one ayah at a time: each ayah is a clip of the surah file, so repeat, the ayah bar, ±10 s and follow stay as they are, and the bismillah is what comes before the first ayah. A surah that isn't downloaded streams from the address (Al-Baqarah is 86 to 165 MB, downloading it before the first sound is minutes of silence), nothing of it is kept. A download keeps the whole file, checked like the ayah files, and the timings are kept next to it, so it plays offline. The timings aren't right for every surah, so `tool/quran/check_surah_reciters.py` checks each one and the wrong ones go in the reciter's `missing`, where the app says the surah isn't in that voice yet. Islam Sobhi has no timings and Abdulrahman Mosad no source with an address, both wait for one.
