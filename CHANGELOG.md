# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

The first release of Better Muslim.

- Prayer times for where you are, worked out on your phone, with the adhan or a soft tone for
  each prayer
- A countdown to the next prayer in your notifications, and widgets for your home screen
- Mark the prayers you prayed, and keep a list of the ones to make up
- The Qibla compass
- The Quran page by page, with recitation, tafsir, translation, bookmarks and notes
- Morning and evening azkar, tasbih and duas
- The Hijri calendar
- Eight themes in light and dark, in English and Arabic
- No account and no ads. Your location, prayers and notes stay on your phone
