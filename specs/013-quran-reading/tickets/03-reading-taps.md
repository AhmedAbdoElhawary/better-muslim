# 03: Reading taps

**What to build:** Turn strips on both sides, any other tap toggles the Reader controls, a long
press or a double tap opens the Ayah sheet with a medium impact, and Share starts choosing.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** done

- [x] A tap within 44 pt of the left edge turns to the next page, the right edge to the previous,
      with the same turn as a swipe, in both languages and with the Reader controls shown.
- [x] A tap anywhere else, words included, shows or hides the Reader controls.
- [x] A long press or a double tap on an ayah opens its Ayah sheet with a medium impact. Opening
      the reader at an ayah has no haptic.
- [x] With a sheet open a tap only closes it. While choosing, a tap on an ayah checks or unchecks
      it at once and the strips still turn.
- [x] Share on the Ayah sheet starts choosing with this ayah checked.
- [x] A screen reader's tap on an ayah opens its sheet, or checks it while choosing.
- [x] Widget tests cover both strips, a tap on the words, a long press, a double tap and Share.
