# 15: Bookmark ribbon in the mushaf margin

**What to build:** A bookmarked ayah shows a small ribbon in the page's side margin, beside the
line where the ayah ends, in its category's color. Nothing is drawn inside the text line.

**Blocked by:** 13

**Status:** done

- [x] The ribbon sits in the side margin beside the ayah's last line, never over the glyphs or
      the page frame.
- [x] Two bookmarks ending on the same line show both colors without overlapping.
- [x] It appears, changes color and goes as soon as a bookmark is added, moved or removed.
- [x] It lines up at every mushaf size, including the bigger sizes that rewrap the page.
- [x] A widget test checks a bookmarked ayah has its ribbon and an unbookmarked one has none.
